# MindSet: Vision — A toolbox for testing DNNs on key psychological experiments

## What it does

A toolbox of image datasets + regeneration scripts to test DNNs (and VLMs) on
**~30 psychological findings**. Its explicit stance: most DNN-vs-human benchmarks
are **observational** (unmanipulated naturalistic images); MindSet instead
**systematically manipulates stimuli to test hypotheses**, with configurable
generation parameters. Includes classic effects; **Weber's law is assessed for
length judgment**.

## Key findings

- Provides controlled, regenerable stimulus sets for hypothesis-driven testing;
  positions manipulation (not naturalistic accuracy) as the right tool.

## Relevance to our artifact

Strong methodological ally and a **partial Weber's-law neighbor** — but Weber for
**length**, not **contrast discrimination**. Confirms Weber-of-contrast is open.
Its "manipulate, don't observe" philosophy is exactly ours; its regenerable-
stimulus design is a model for our stimulus generator.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite as kindred methodology and as the reason our Weber claim is scoped to
**contrast discrimination** (not length). Consider adopting/citing its stimulus-
generation conventions.
