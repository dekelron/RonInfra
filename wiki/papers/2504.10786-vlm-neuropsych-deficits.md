# Visual Language Models show widespread visual deficits on neuropsychological tests

- **Authors:** (confirm in PDF)
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv 2025
- **ID:** arXiv:2504.10786
- **Cluster:** G — VLM deficits wave
- **Sourcing:** `snippet`

## What it does

Administers standardized **neuropsychological / clinical visual tests** to VLMs
and catalogues where they fail relative to human norms.

## Key findings

- **Widespread** low-/mid-level visual deficits across models, even where
  high-level reasoning looks strong.

## Relevance to our artifact

Defines the **deficit-cataloguing** framing we must *avoid*. It documents *that*
VLMs fail; our contribution is *how much / where the threshold sits / and can we
move it* — measurement + causation, not a failure list.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite as evidence of low-level fragility motivating precise psychophysics.
Differentiate on **parametric thresholds + causal manipulation** vs. pass/fail
test batteries.
