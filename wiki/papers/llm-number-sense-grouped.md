# LLM number-sense / magnitude representation — grouped notes

- **Cluster:** H — magnitude/number in LLMs
- **Sourcing:** `snippet` for all; verify before citing

Grouped: these study **numeric magnitude** representation in language models —
the *symbolic* analogue of our sensory transducer. Context for Cacioli
(2603.20642), and the boundary our niche sits just outside of.

## Members

- **Number Representations in LLMs: A Computational Parallel to Human Perception**
  (arXiv:2502.16147, 2024/25; El-Shangiti et al.) — geometry of numerical
  reasoning; LLMs compare numeric properties in **linear subspaces**; parallels
  to human number perception.
- **What is a Number, That a Large Language Model May Know It?**
  (arXiv:2502.01540, 2025) — how LLMs encode/represent numbers (per-digit vs.
  magnitude; circular vs. linear vs. log structure).
- **Large-scale Generative AI Models Lack Visual Number Sense**
  (arXiv:2402.03328, 2024) — generative/multimodal models fail human-like
  **subitizing / approximate number** in images.
- Background: Gurnee & Tegmark 2024 (LLMs represent space and time); "neural basis
  of the Weber-Fechner law: a logarithmic mental number line" (Nieder-line,
  human/neuro).

## Relevance to our artifact

Establishes that **log/Weber magnitude coding in transformers is an active,
partly-claimed area** — but in the **symbolic/numeric** domain. Our niche is the
**sensory visual (contrast/luminance)** transducer through the vision encoder.
2402.03328 also shows *visual* number sense is weak — adjacent, not our variable.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite to bound our claim ("magnitude-in-LLMs is studied; the *sensory visual*
transducer is not"). Reinforces the Cacioli differentiation (modality/pathway).
