# Getting aligned on representational alignment

- **Authors:** large multi-author collaboration (Sucholutsky et al.)
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv 2023 (position/survey)
- **ID:** arXiv:2310.13018
- **Cluster:** D — human/DNN perception foundations (framing)
- **Sourcing:** `snippet`

## What it does

A synthesis/position piece unifying vocabulary and methods for **representational
alignment** — comparing internal representations across biological and artificial
systems (RSA, CKA, probing, etc.).

## Relevance to our artifact

Frames our **representational surface** (encoder-RDM replication of the 2017 log
law). Provides shared terminology (RSA / dissimilarity matrices) we will use, and
situates the behaviour↔representation comparison methodologically.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite when introducing RSA and the representational surface. Differentiate: we do
not stop at alignment measurement — we add **behaviour** and a **causal**
intervention to test *mechanism*, not just similarity.
