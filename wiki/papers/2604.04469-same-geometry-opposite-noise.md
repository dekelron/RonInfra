# Same Geometry, Opposite Noise: Transformer Magnitude Representations Lack Scalar Variability

- **Author:** Jon-Paul Cacioli (independent researcher, Melbourne) — follow-up to
  2603.20642
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv, 2026
- **ID:** arXiv:2604.04469 ✓ verified
- **Cluster:** B — direct neighbor (mechanism/identifiability)
- **Sourcing:** `verified` (via search; abstract + key numbers confirmed)

## What it does

Follow-up to the transformer-Weber's-law work. Examines the **noise structure**
of transformer magnitude representations, not just their geometry. Central
distinction: **scalar variability** (representational noise grows proportionally
with magnitude → constant coefficient of variation, CV — the hallmark of
biological magnitude systems) vs. non-scalar noise. Method: dispersion of
hidden-state reps across **carrier sentences** for **26 numerical magnitudes** in
three 7–8B models (Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Llama-3-8B-Base).
**Numeric/token magnitude only — no visual stimulus at all.**

## Key findings (verified)

- Transformers reproduce **log-compressive geometry** but show the **opposite
  noise signature**: representational variability **decreases** with magnitude
  (scaling exponent **α ≈ −0.19**; **0 of 16** primary layers with α > 0, across
  all three models). Biology has α ≈ 0 (constant CV). "Same geometry, opposite
  noise."

## Relevance to our artifact — this is the theoretical hook

Direct evidence for the **identifiability problem** at the center of our
contribution: **a Weber-like behavioural signature and log geometry do NOT
uniquely pin down the mechanism** — noise structure is a separate, dissociable
axis. Our dual-surface + causal design is exactly what's needed to test, in the
**sensory/contrast** domain, whether the log response is genuine log-encoding +
constant noise or linear-encoding + scalar noise.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite as motivation for measuring **noise/variability**, not just mean transducer
shape. This paper gives our primary novelty a **concrete, falsifiable form**:
2604.04469 answered the noise-signature question for *numeric* magnitude (result:
non-biological, α≈−0.19). **Nobody has asked it for a *sensory* (contrast)
transducer in a VLM.** So our headline question becomes: *does the VLM contrast
transducer carry biological constant-CV (scalar-variability) noise, or the
transformer-like decreasing-CV signature — and does a causal LoRA move it?* That
is the one question no neighbor has touched in vision.
