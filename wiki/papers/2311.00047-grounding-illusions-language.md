# Grounding Visual Illusions in Language: Do Vision-Language Models Perceive Illusions Like Humans?

- **Authors:** (confirm in PDF)

## What it does

Introduces a dataset with **natural-language annotations** of visual illusions to
test whether VLMs' responses align with human illusory percepts.

## Key findings

- Partial, model-dependent alignment with human illusions; larger/newer models
  sometimes align more; a first NL-grounded illusion evaluation for VLMs.
