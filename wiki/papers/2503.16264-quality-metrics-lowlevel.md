# Do image and video quality metrics model low-level human vision?

- **Authors:** (confirm in PDF)
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv 2025
- **ID:** arXiv:2503.16264
- **Cluster:** G — low-level vision evaluation
- **Sourcing:** `snippet`

## What it does

Evaluates image/video **quality metrics** against **psychophysical** low-level
vision data using **detection and masking** paradigms and a **contrast-threshold
alignment** score (do metrics predict equal quality at the contrast threshold?).

## Key findings

- Many quality metrics only partially follow low-level psychophysics (contrast
  thresholds/masking).

## Relevance to our artifact

A clean example of **contrast-threshold psychophysics used to audit models**, and
a source of **masking/detection** protocols and alignment scoring we can borrow.
Metric-focused, not VLM-behaviour — complementary, not competing.

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite for contrast-threshold/masking methodology. Differentiate: metrics vs. **VLM
behaviour + encoder + causal**; detection/masking vs. **suprathreshold
transducer**.
