## What it does

Tests whether transformer LMs represent magnitude logarithmically. Uses **four
converging paradigms** — representational similarity analysis (RSA), behavioural
discrimination, precision gradients, and **causal intervention** — across **three
magnitude domains (numerical, temporal, spatial)** in **three 7–9B
instruction-tuned models** spanning three families (Llama, Mistral, Qwen).
Motivated by **efficient coding**: verifies the precondition that integer
frequencies in training data follow a **power law**.

## Key findings

- **Log-compressive** representational geometry: RSA correlation with a Weber-law
  dissimilarity matrix **.68–.96** across all 96 model×domain×layer cells; linear
  geometry never preferred.
- **Behaviour/geometry dissociation:** one model gives a human-range Weber
  fraction (WF = 0.20), another does not; both at chance on temporal/spatial
  discrimination *despite* having log geometry there.
- **Layer dissociation:** early layers (weakest geometry) are causally implicated
  in approximate magnitude; later layers (strongest geometry) are not causally
  engaged.
