# ImageNet-trained DNN exhibits illusion-like response to the Scintillating Grid

- **Authors:** Eric D. Sun, Ron Dekel
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv, Jul 2019; a version appears in **JOV (Journal of
  Vision)** — "ImageNet-trained deep neural networks exhibit illusion-like
  response to the Scintillating grid"
- **ID:** arXiv:1907.09019
- **Cluster:** A — prior work (the lineage)
- **Sourcing:** `snippet`

## What it does

Characterizes VGG-19's **representational dissimilarity** as a function of
dot-whiteness in the Scintillating Grid illusion (white dots perceived as
partially black). Representational, not behavioural.

## Key findings

- A significant deviation from the expected **monotonic** relation between
  representational dissimilarity and dot-whiteness: a **non-monotonic** response
  — dissimilarity rises then *falls* as whiteness increases — echoing the
  illusion.
