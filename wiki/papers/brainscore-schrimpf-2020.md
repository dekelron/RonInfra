## What it does

**Brain-Score:** a large composite of **neural + behavioural** benchmarks scoring
how well a model matches the primate ventral visual stream (neural areas +
object-recognition behaviour). Guided development of **CORnet-S**, a shallow
recurrent ANN with mapped areas that scores highly while staying compact.

## Key findings

- A single leaderboard integrating many labs' neural/behavioural data;
  ImageNet accuracy and brain-similarity partly dissociate; recurrence helps.

## Relevance to our artifact

The reference point for **"benchmark = alignment to biology."** We are
deliberately **not** that: our thesis is measurement methodology + a causal probe,
**not** "VLMs are good models of the brain." Human alignment is a comparison axis,
never the claim (positioning rule; red-team item #5).

## How we cite / differentiate

Cite to locate ourselves: Brain-Score = neural/behavioural *alignment* scoring of
*vision models*; ours = *behavioural psychophysics of a transducer law* in *VLMs*
with causal manipulation. Credit; do not compete on their axis.
