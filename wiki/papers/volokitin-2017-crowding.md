# Do Deep Neural Networks Suffer from Crowding?

- **Authors:** Anna Volokitin, Gemma Roig, Tomaso Poggio
- **Venue / Year:** NeurIPS 2017 (poster)
- **ID:** arXiv:1706.08616 ✓ verified
- **Cluster:** E — crowding (prior art)
- **Sourcing:** `snippet` (id/venue verified; method detail still snippet-grade)

## What it does

Tests whether DNNs exhibit **crowding** — impaired target recognition with nearby
flankers — as a function of flanker configuration, spacing, eccentricity, and
whether the model has seen flankers in training.

## Key findings (as understood)

- DNNs show crowding-like impairments dependent on flanker similarity/spacing and
  pooling; behaviour differs from humans in configuration sensitivity.
